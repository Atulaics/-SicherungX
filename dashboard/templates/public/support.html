Frequently Asked Questions

How do I deploy the agent silently?

Enterprise customers can deploy `SicherungAgent.exe` via SCCM or Intune using the `--hidden` and `--auto-login` flags.

Does the agent work offline?

Yes. If the endpoint loses connection to the SaaS backend, the agent caches logs locally and uploads them sequentially once the connection is restored.

How is the AI model trained?

The Isolation Forest model is pre-trained on generic insider threat behavior, and fine-tunes itself automatically based on the rolling 7-day baseline of your organization.
